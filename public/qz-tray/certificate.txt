=== SETUP INSTRUCTIONS (for app developer) ===

1. Open QZ Tray on your computer (system tray icon)
2. Go to Advanced > Site Manager
3. Click the + button to create a new certificate
4. A folder "QZ Tray Demo Cert" will appear on your Desktop
5. Copy the ENTIRE contents of digital-certificate.txt here
6. Copy the private-key.pem contents to your server's environment variable:
   QZ_TRAY_PRIVATE_KEY="-----BEGIN PRIVATE KEY-----\n...\n-----END PRIVATE KEY-----"
   (Replace actual newlines with \n)

=== USER SETUP (for your customers) ===

Once you've set up the certificate, your users need to:
1. Install QZ Tray from https://qz.io/download/
2. Download this certificate file from your app
3. Import it into QZ Tray:
   - Open QZ Tray > Advanced > Site Manager
   - Click the + button
   - Select "Browse" and choose this certificate file
   - Click "Trust" or "Allow"

After this one-time setup, printing works silently!
